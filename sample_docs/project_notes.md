# Project Atlas – Project Notes

## Overview
Project Atlas is a support-ticket assistant for our client, Nordlicht Logistics GmbH.
The assistant reads incoming customer support tickets, sorts them by topic, and
suggests a first reply for the support team to review.

## Team
- Engagement lead: Maria Keller
- Technical lead: Jonas Weber
- Client contact: Dr. Lena Fischer (Head of Customer Service, Nordlicht Logistics)

## Timeline
- Kick-off: 2 November 2026
- Pilot with 5 support agents: 15 January 2027
- Target go-live date: 1 March 2027

## Scope
### Phase 1 (until go-live)
- Ticket intake from email only
- Automatic topic sorting (delivery, billing, damage, other)
- Suggested first replies in German and English

### Phase 2 (after go-live)
- Ticket intake from Slack
- Weekly summary report for the support manager

## Constraints
- All ticket data must stay inside the client's own environment.
  No ticket data may be sent to or stored on external servers.
- Every suggested reply must be reviewed by a human agent before it is sent.
